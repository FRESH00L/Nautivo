# Polish sailing and water-activity market: customers, counts, prices, seasonality

Verification date: 2026-11-05. Scope: Poland. Sources were checked on that date.

Evidence tags:
- **[V]** = I opened the page or document and read the figure there.
- **[V-calc]** = my arithmetic on a [V] figure.

## How many sailing clubs, associations and PZŻ members exist?

### Takeaway
PZŻ counted 553 members at 31.12.2024: 35 regional unions plus 518 other organisations, with 22,158 people in the membership table. This is the best sourced count of organisations. It is shrinking (547 to 518 organisations in 2024), and it does not cover clubs outside PZŻ. No official count of all sailing clubs was found.

### Cited Findings
- At 31.12.2024 PZŻ had 553 members. 463 were ordinary: 35 okręgowe związki żeglarskie (OZŻ), 21 class associations, 5 Polish-diaspora clubs and 402 other organisations. 90 were supporting: 7 class associations and 83 other organisations. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, p. 2](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- The membership table shows organisations / people by year: 2021 524 / 22,314; 2022 537 / 22,632; 2023 547 / 23,562; 2024 518 / 22,158. In 2024, 429 organisations (18,894 people) were grouped in OZŻ and 89 (3,264 people) directly in PZŻ. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, p. 3](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- 518 + 35 OZŻ = 553, and 402 + 5 + 21 + 83 + 7 = 518, so the narrative and the table reconcile. [V-calc]
- Largest regions in 2024, organisations / people: Pomorski ZŻ 54 / 3,515; Śląski ZŻ 50 / 2,562; Zachodniopomorski 49 / 1,711; Warszawsko-Mazowiecki 28 / 1,541; Wielkopolski 27 / 1,909; Warmińsko-Mazurski (the Masurian region) 22 / 670. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, p. 3](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- Churn in 2024: 11 admitted (6 ordinary, 5 supporting), 31 expelled for unpaid dues and/or unmet formal conditions, 7 resigned, 2 dissolved. Net −29 matches the table's fall from 547 to 518. [V, V-calc] — [PZŻ 2024 report, pp. 9-10](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- Members include commercial operators, for example "Czarter Powidz Maciej Krajka" and "Krystian Szypka Ocean Team Sailing School" (both admitted as supporting members in 2024). PZŻ refused MMorska Sp. z o.o. as an ordinary member because its articles lacked sailing activity and it had no OZŻ membership. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, p. 9](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- 128 PZŻ sports-club licences were current at 31.12.2024; a club licence is a separate status from membership. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, p. 74](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- The Ministry-funded PGE PolSailing programme ran at 47 centres in 31 towns in 2024. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- PZŻ already runs its own member systems: ebiuro.pya.org.pl (member organisations, licences) and events.pya.org.pl. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- GUS, 2016 (latest sailing breakdown I could read): 250 sailing sections in sports clubs with 7,606 people practising. Of these, sailing 211 / 6,850, windsurfing 23 / 522, ice yachting 12 / 177. Sections are not clubs, and the data is 10 years old. [V] — [GUS Kultura fizyczna 2015-2016, Table 10](https://stat.gov.pl/files/gfx/portalinformacyjny/pl/defaultaktualnosci/5495/1/4/1/kultura_fizyczna_w_polsce_w_latach_2015_i_2016.pdf)
- GUS, 2022: 14,476 sports clubs of all disciplines; 6.2% of people practising did water sports, with no sailing split in the signal publication. [V] — [GUS Kultura fizyczna w 2022](https://stat.gov.pl/files/gfx/portalinformacyjny/pl/defaultaktualnosci/5495/13/6/1/kultura_fizyczna_w_2022_r..pdf)
- GUS, 2024: 113.1 thousand NGOs, of which 17.8% are sports associations. [V] — [GUS, Działalność organizacji pozarządowych w 2024 r.](https://stat.gov.pl/obszary-tematyczne/gospodarka-spoleczna-wolontariat/gospodarka-spoleczna-trzeci-sektor/dzialalnosc-organizacji-pozarzadowych-w-2024-r-,3,11.html)
- The 2017 PZŻ report appears to give 753 ordinary members. [S, OCR-damaged scan] — [PZŻ 2017 report](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2017.pdf)
- Pomorski ZŻ reported 59 clubs and sections and 3,338 people at end-2022. [S] — [PoZŻ 2022 report](https://zeglarski.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2023.03.26-Sprawozdanie-PoZZ-2022-calosc.pdf)

### Inferences
- The average PZŻ organisation is small: 22,158 / 518 = about 43 people. Warmińsko-Mazurski is smaller still at 670 / 22 = about 30. This is a mean, and the distribution is not published.
- The Masurian OZŻ has only 22 organisations despite being the main charter region. Masurian demand therefore looks commercial rather than club-based.
- 113.1k × 17.8% = about 20,100 sports associations. This is context only and must not be used as a sailing ceiling.
- If the 2017 figure holds, ordinary membership fell from about 753 to 463. The cause is unknown; some of it may be register cleaning, given the 2024 expulsions.

### Gaps
- KRS/REGON counts of sailing associations: not obtained. Neither register was queried for the name "żeglarski" or by PKD. No published statistic was found. A paid or manual register query would be needed.
- Clubs outside PZŻ: number unknown.
- GUS sailing sections after 2016: not found in the documents I could read. The tables are likely in GUS XLSX files I did not open.
- PZMWiNW member clubs: number not found in the 2024 board report.
- Public list of PZŻ member organisations: not retrieved.

## How many sailing schools or course organisers exist, and who may run patent courses?

### Takeaway
Under the rules I read, no licence or course is required to train people for żeglarz jachtowy or sternik motorowodny. Only exams are regulated. There is therefore no official list of course organisers. The closest proxy is the Ministry list of authorised exam entities at 05.10.2026: 518 decisions, about 210 distinct organisations.

### Cited Findings
- Exams are run by the relevant Polish sports association or an entity authorised by the minister; art. 37a of the inland navigation act is the basis. [S for the act's wording, V for the regulation below] — [Standardy Prawa](https://standardyprawa.pl/akt/413/art/48634)
- The water tourism regulation sets the żeglarz jachtowy (§5) and sternik motorowodny (§8) conditions as age 14 and a passed exam. Exam admission (§14) requires meeting general conditions and paying the fee, with no course requirement. The regulation does not address who may run training. [V] — [Dz.U. 2013 poz. 460](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2013/460/text.html)
- Authorisation requires an application to the minister, at least 4 qualified examiners and documented facilities (§2). Examiner requirements are in §3. [V] — [Dz.U. 2013 poz. 602](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2013/602/text.html)
- Only PZŻ issues sailing patents and only PZMWiNW issues motorboat patents, per one authorised school's summary. [V, secondary] — [Szkoła Żeglarstwa Trawers](https://www.trawers.waw.pl/egzaminy/124-egzaminy-i-patenty)
- The Ministry publishes "Wykaz podmiotów uprawnionych – stan na 05.10.2026 r."; the page was last modified 05.10.2026. [V] — [gov.pl egzaminy żeglarskie](https://www.gov.pl/web/sport/egzaminy-zeglarskie)
- The list has 518 numbered rows, one per decision, dated 2013 to 2026. [V] — [Wykaz, 05.10.2026](https://www.gov.pl/attachment/d9006683-f406-4719-9573-c25adb44cbb5)
- My parse of that PDF [V-calc; automated name matching, so approximate]:
  - Distinct normalised names: 210. Distinct postal codes: 169. Distinct name + postal code: 218.
  - Sail exam rights (żeglarz jachtowy or jachtowy sternik morski): 174. Motorboat rights: 176. Both: 142.
  - By patent: żeglarz jachtowy 168, jachtowy sternik morski 118, sternik motorowodny 173, motorowodny sternik morski 101.
  - Legal form by keyword, of 210: associations, clubs, WOPR, ZHP and similar 56; sp. z o.o. 33; universities and public bodies 3; other, mostly sole traders or unclear, 118.
  - 22 names have addresses in a list of Masurian towns (Giżycko 6, Mikołajki 4, Ruciane-Nida 3, others). This is by seat, not by where they operate.
- Share of exams by authorised entities, 2024: 80.89% of żeglarz jachtowy and 89.19% of jachtowy sternik morski patents. PZŻ-route counts were 2,564 and 257; authorised entities 10,854 and 2,121; totals 13,418 and 2,378. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, p. 71](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- Earlier żeglarz jachtowy / jachtowy sternik morski totals: 2021 13,272 / 2,107; 2022 12,465 / 2,473; 2023 12,936 / 2,513. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, p. 71](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- OZŻ-run exams, 2024: 245 żeglarz jachtowy sessions with 2,723 candidates and 2,539 passes. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, p. 70](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- Motorboat qualifications issued by PZMWiNW in 2024 totalled 70,473, described as a record. After exams at authorised entities: 49,777 sternik motorowodny and 703 motorowodny sternik morski. After exams at PZMWiNW centres: 9,980 and 133. All-grade exam-based issuances were 53,865 via authorised entities and 11,021 via PZMWiNW. [V] — [PZMWiNW board report 2024, p. 7](https://www.motorowodniacy.org/dokumenty/statut_i_akty_prawne/sprawozdanie_z_prac_zarzadu_pzmwinw_za_okres_od_01012024_do_31122024_r.pdf)
- PZMWiNW says exams keep moving to authorised entities, that training is falling or absent, and that mandatory pre-exam training should return. This is a position, not current law. [V] — [PZMWiNW board report 2024, p. 7](https://www.motorowodniacy.org/dokumenty/statut_i_akty_prawne/sprawozdanie_z_prac_zarzadu_pzmwinw_za_okres_od_01012024_do_31122024_r.pdf)
- PZŻ trainer licences current at 31.12.2024: 380. These are sports trainers, not all course instructors. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, p. 74](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- Older unofficial list: about 79 organisations at 31.05.2017. [V, unofficial, page reports a database error] — [edukacjazeglarska.pl](https://edukacjazeglarska.pl/index.php/podmioty-egzaminacyjne/podmioty-upowaznione)

### Inferences
- Motorboat patent volume is about 4.5 times sailing: 59,757 sternik motorowodny exam issuances vs 13,418 żeglarz jachtowy. Motor course and exam operators may be a larger or faster-growing segment than sailing schools.
- "No licence for training" is my reading from the regulation's silence. A lawyer should confirm it, and it should be checked against any consolidated text.
- 210 entities understate course organisers (schools can train without exam rights) and overstate them (some entities may hold exam rights only, or be dormant). The list has no activity flag.
- PZMWiNW's report of low or absent training suggests that many motor candidates do not buy a full course. The share is unknown.

### Gaps
- Count of schools and course organisers: not found; none appears to be officially kept.
- Public list of PZMWiNW authorised centres: not retrieved.
- Legal changes after 2019 to regulation 460: not fully checked against a consolidated text.
- Whether all 518 decisions are still active: not stated in the list.

### Inferences
- Charter bookings are worth about twice a course and are paid as two transfers, which affects per-payment fees.
- Cruise value on the platform may understate customer spend, because kitty, transit log and similar costs are paid separately (about EUR 250 per person per week at Yachtica).

The share who buy a course is unknown, and PZMWiNW suggests many take little or no training, so these are not market size estimates.

## When are bookings and payments made? What are the seasonal data and channels?

### Takeaway
Masurian tourism concentrates in July and August, and charter pricing peaks in the same window. Published terms show deposits at booking and balances about four weeks before departure, which implies payments before the season. No source measures booking lead times. Boat show figures are old or partial.

## What is the evidence on willingness to pay, and how do small clubs budget for software?

### Takeaway
Unknown. I found no evidence on software budgets of Polish sailing clubs or associations. Only indirect signals exist: typical NGO budgets are small, and some PZŻ members fail to pay dues.

### Cited Findings
- Klon/Jawor (random sample of 1,012 associations and other NGOs, May 2025): median NGO revenue in 2023 was PLN 50,000; the median association has 25 members; 34% work in sport, tourism, recreation or hobby; 66% use paid work; 19% employ someone on a contract of employment; 35% have any financial reserve. This is not sailing-specific. [V] — [Kondycja organizacji pozarządowych 2024](https://api.ngo.pl/media/get/258002)
- 31 PZŻ members were expelled in 2024 for unpaid dues and/or unmet formal conditions. Proceedings for unpaid dues also touched OZŻ, including Warmińsko-Mazurski. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report, pp. 9-10](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- PZŻ provides central systems for membership and licensing, so these functions may not be something members pay separately for. [V] — [PZŻ 2024 report](https://pya.org.pl/files/sprawozdania/sprawozdanie-z-dzialalnosci-pzz-za-rok-2024.pdf)
- International competitors exist (Booking Manager, Andronautic, Rezdy, Anolla, Charterfy), mostly with no public price in the snippets. Roverd advertises no monthly fees. These are vendor claims. [S] — [Roverd](https://www.roverd.com/sailing-charter-booking-software), [Anolla PL](https://anolla.com/pl/oprogramowanie-do-wynajmu-lodzi), [Rezdy](https://rezdy.com/charter/)

### Inferences
- A club close to the NGO median has about PLN 50,000 a year in total revenue, so fixed fees are likely to face resistance. This is reasoning from a general NGO figure, not evidence about sailing clubs. Clubs with marinas or fleets may differ considerably.
- The dues evidence shows payment friction with a federation, not with software.

### Gaps

- Price sensitivity between subscription and commission: unknown. Interviews or a pricing test are needed.
- Median revenues specific to sailing clubs: unknown. Public benefit organisation (OPP) filings and KRS financial statements could provide a sample.

## What is the addressable number of organisations?

### Takeaway
Known, countable organisations: **548 to 936**. This is a range of named or listed entities, not a total market. The width comes from unmeasured overlap, and the range omits cruise organisers and non-PZŻ clubs, so the true population could be higher. ### Inferences
- The floor of 548 is surely too low in reality, since 118 of the 210 exam entities appear to be sole traders or otherwise unclear, and PZŻ rejected at least one company for formal reasons. However, I measured no overlap, so I give no midpoint.
