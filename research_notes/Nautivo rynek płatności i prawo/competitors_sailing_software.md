# Sailing and boating management software competitors relevant to Nautivo

Verification date: 2026-11-05. All vendor facts reflect what the pages showed on that date.

How to read these notes:
- CONFIRMED means read directly on the vendor's site at the cited URL. Vendor statements are the vendor's claims (customer counts, testimonials), not independently verified.
- THIRD-PARTY means a directory, a competitor's blog or a search-result summary. The page may be out of date, and some were not opened in full.
- INFERENCE means my own reasoning. All "Nautivo advantage" points are inferences.
- "Not mentioned" means absent from the pages read. It does not prove the feature is absent from the product.

Method warning that affects every "not found" below: my Polish-language searches did not surface Boater, Portivo's product site or yachtCMS as vendors. Portivo appeared only once, through a blog post. Boater and yachtCMS were found only by testing domain names taken from the brief. The search tool is also US-based. Other small Polish products may therefore exist that these searches missed.

## Which products exist and are active, and what do they do?

### Takeaway
Three Polish-language charter-fleet systems are live and sell today: Boater, Portivo and yachtCMS. Booking Manager (MMK) and NauSYS are also in use by Polish operators, so bareboat rental in Poland is contested. Of the names in the brief, a "SailUp" sailing-management product and "SEARM" could not be found.

#### Polish-language charter systems

**Boater** — [boater.pl](https://boater.pl)

CONFIRMED:
- Self-description: a reservation and fleet management system for charter fleets, "stworzony przez armatorów". It covers motorboats, sailing yachts, houseboats and RIBs.
- Features: an online booking wizard on the operator's own domain, a fleet calendar with multiple marinas, hourly slots, full payment or a 30% deposit with automatic reminders, and expiry of unpaid bookings.
- Documents and compliance: automatic rental contracts, VAT, advance and final invoices with KSeF, corrections on cancellation, and a GDPR module with an audit log.
- Customer side: self-service without a password, and a 360-degree pre-rental safety video with a watch check. Skipper service, route maps and padlock codes are also listed.
- Stated integrations: Przelewy24, PayU, BLIK, Fakturownia, inFakt, KSeF, SMSAPI, Google Maps, the Biała Lista / GUS data lookup, and an open API.
- Pricing: 299 zł net per month + 1.5% of transactions paid online, or Lifetime at 4,500 zł net one-off with 0% commission and one year of updates and support. Both have no limit on boats or bookings. Optional add-ons: implementation package +1,200 zł, website from 3,600 zł.
- Courses, clubs and berth sales are not mentioned. The "szkolenia online" are pre-rental safety videos, not sailing courses.
- The claim "jedyny tak rozbudowany system na rynku czarterowym" is the vendor's own.

Limits and unknowns:
- Testimonials use first names only (Marcin, Agnieszka, Tomasz) and cannot be verified.
- I found no legal entity, NIP or KRS number in the page text I extracted.
- The phone number shown, +48 600 100 200, looks like a placeholder (INFERENCE).
- Customer count: unknown.

INFERENCE: The product looks focused on short-term motorboat and houseboat rental. Maturity cannot be judged from outside and needs a demo.

**Portivo** — [home](https://operator.portivo.eu/), [pricing PL](https://operator.portivo.eu/pl/cennik), [about](https://operator.portivo.eu/pl/about), [security](https://operator.portivo.eu/pl/security)

CONFIRMED:
- Self-description: "software for charter companies". Modules: bookings and calendar, deposits and instalments, a booking website, a mobile app, POS, identity verification (document and selfie), handover reports, online offers, fleet and service management, marketing and CRM.
- On the English home page the store apps are labelled "Coming to App Store / Google Play" and POS "Coming soon", so parts of the product are not fully released.
- Legal and data: the service is provided by Portivo Inc., based in the USA, so Polish customers account for VAT by reverse charge. The EU representative is Outofplace Poland sp. z o.o., Łomża. Data is listed as AWS Frankfurt, and payments run through Stripe.
- Team named: Marcel Bogdan (CEO), Filip Maszota (CTO), Wojciech Sieradzan (COO).
- Pricing: 14-day trial, then the Fleet plan at 149 zł net per month + 3% of online payments, minimum 15 zł per booking, with no limit on yachts or bookings. Branded customer app: +249 zł per month. Enterprise: custom.
- Stripe fees are charged on top: EEA card 1.5% + 1 zł, BLIK 1.6% + 1 zł, chargeback 90 zł per dispute plus 90 zł per response (stated as at 3 October 2026). Cash, transfer and terminal carry 0% Portivo commission.
- The 3% is presented as covering identity verification, SMS and transactional email.
- Courses, clubs and berth sales are not mentioned on the pricing page or the Polish product page.

THIRD-PARTY: A search summary said Portivo's blog mentions KSeF. I could not confirm this when reading the blog page directly, so treat KSeF as unconfirmed.

INFERENCE: The page also shows "398 zł" next to 149 zł. This equals 149 + 249, so it is probably the price with the branded app.

**yachtCMS** — [home](https://yachtcms.pl), [pricing](https://yachtcms.pl/cennik), [about](https://yachtcms.pl/o-nas)

CONFIRMED:
- Self-description: a platform for charter companies with a calendar widget for the operator's site (WordPress, Joomla, Wix, HTML) and an API.
- Features: online deposits (own bank account or Przelewy24), charter contract generation, overbooking protection, bosun reports, SMS alerts, discount codes, a paid channel manager and portal integration including zegluj.pl. A reviews widget is marked "coming soon".
- Track record claims: "14 lat doświadczeń", from ResGo.pl, and "ponad 200 firm czarterowych i armatorów". Also yachts, boats, houseboats and floating apartments, and previous marina berth reservation work.
- Pricing: prepaid points that never expire. One active yacht costs 1 point per day. A point costs 0.20–0.40 zł net; packs run from 375 points for 150 zł to 25,000 points for 5,000 zł. New accounts get 50 free points.
- No commission on bookings. The site positions this against "inne firmy" charging 1.5–3%.
- Courses, clubs and berth sales are not mentioned on the pages read.

### Inferences
My own arithmetic, for illustration only. Net of VAT and excluding payment-provider fees. Assumes 5 yachts and 200,000 zł per year paid online.
- Boater subscription: 299 × 12 + 1.5% × 200,000 = about 6,588 zł per year.
- Boater Lifetime: 4,500 zł once, with only the first year of updates and support included.
- Portivo: 149 × 12 + 3% × 200,000 = about 7,788 zł per year.
- yachtCMS: 5 × 365 = 1,825 points. At 0.30 zł per point that is about 548 zł per year for the base functions, before premium options.

What this suggests:
- A large price gap exists in Poland, and small-fleet buyers have a very cheap anchor in yachtCMS.
- A percentage model competes against a 0% message that is already on the market. Yacht charters have high booking values, so a percentage is felt more than in low-value day rental.
- Seasonal, course and club customers may need different pricing logic, since dues and low-value course sales behave differently from charters.

### Gaps
- Discounts, negotiated pricing and real invoices: unknown.
- Clubspot's current rates from the vendor itself: not rendered on the pricing page.

## Which niches appear unserved in Poland, and what is the evidence?

### Takeaway
Bareboat and short-term rental is not an unserved niche. Per-berth cruises, sailing-course management and sailing-club management look like gaps in Polish-language software, but the evidence is absence from searches plus one club's practice, so it does not establish low competition.

### Inferences
- The gap most likely to be real is a single Polish system combining courses, per-berth cruises and rental for businesses that do all three. [NaRejsy.pl](https://www.nausys.com/?p=21719), for example, offers charter, training and corporate events, while running NauSYS for its fleet.
- An unserved niche may reflect low willingness to pay rather than an opening. Forms and spreadsheets are free, and clubs are often volunteer-run. No evidence on budgets was found.
- Existing Polish vendors could extend into these niches. Boater and Portivo already hold payments, documents and calendars, so any lead may be short-lived.

### Gaps
Do not claim low competition until these are done:
- Interview 10–15 Polish cruise organisers, schools and clubs about their current tools and spending.
- Search manually on Google from Poland, and check Facebook sailing groups and the footers of operators' booking pages for "powered by" links.
- Check horizontal Polish booking tools and any further Polish vendors that these searches may have missed.
- Book demos with Boater and Portivo to confirm their roadmap and any undocumented cruise or course functions.
